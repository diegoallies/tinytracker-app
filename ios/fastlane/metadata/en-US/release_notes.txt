Reminders that reach everyone caring for your baby.

• Feed and nap reminders are now sent from our servers instead of being
  scheduled on a single phone. If your nanny logs a feed, your reminder
  updates too - even if you haven't opened the app.
• New alert when a caregiver submits a weekly report, so you know it's
  ready to read.
• Overdue alerts follow up if a feed or nap is running late.
• Reminder settings now follow your account across devices.
• Improved stability, with crash reporting added so problems get fixed faster.

Requires iOS 15 or later.
