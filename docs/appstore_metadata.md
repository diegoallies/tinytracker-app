## Description (4000 chars max)

TinyTrack is the fastest way for tired parents to log a feed, a nap or a
nappy — and the smartest way to see the pattern behind them.

Built by a dad for his own newborn, TinyTrack turns the paper tracking pack
your nanny or clinic gave you into one beautiful app that the whole family
shares.

EFFORTLESS DAILY TRACKING
• Feedings — breast, bottle or solids, with a live timer, feed quality and
  spit-up flags
• Sleep — one-tap start/stop with a live timer, backdating and wake windows
• Nappies — wet/dirty/mixed with colour and stool-type reference scales
• Tummy time with daily goals, plus photos, health notes and milestones
• Growth charts on WHO percentile curves

KNOWS WHAT'S NEXT
TinyTrack learns your baby's own rhythm — not textbook averages — and shows
"next up" predictions for the next feeding and nap, daily summaries, and
weekly pattern insights.

THE CARE PACK, DIGITISED
• Reflux tracker with severity scale, triggers and a 14-day trend view
• Daily journal — mood, cramps and gas scales, activities and firsts
• Weekly report matched to your baby's age, with the numbers auto-filled
  from your logs — share it as a PDF or text
• Monthly milestone review checklists
• Care guide with red flags and a tap-to-call emergency contact sheet

BUILT FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Invite grandparents, partners or a nanny by link, with owner, logger and
  viewer roles
• Everyone sees the same live timeline — no more "when was the last feed?"
  texts
• Feeding reminders, offline support and PDF export

APPLE WATCH COMPANION
Log feeds, sleep and nappies straight from your wrist, see the live
schedule at a glance, and check today's summary without picking up your
phone — perfect for 3 a.m. feeds.

DESIGNED FOR NIGHT FEEDS
A proper dark mode, big touch targets and one-handed logging — because you
have a baby in the other arm.

Your data belongs to you: export everything as a PDF at any time, and
delete your account (and all its data) right from the app.
