# TinyTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 July 2026_

TinyTrack ("the app") is a baby tracking app that helps parents and caregivers log feeds, sleeps, nappies, growth, photos and related care information. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

- **Account information:** your email address and optional display name, phone number, bio and profile photo.
- **Baby care data you enter:** baby profiles (name, date of birth, photos) and the records you log — feedings, sleeps, nappy changes, growth measurements, health logs, milestones, medications, journal entries, tummy time, reflux events, immunisations, emergency contacts and photos.

We do not collect location data, advertising identifiers, or analytics that track you across other apps.

## How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features: syncing your records across your devices (including Apple Watch), generating summaries and trends, and sharing a baby's records with caregivers **you** explicitly invite. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

## Where your data is stored

Data is stored securely with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), our backend provider, with access restricted so only you and caregivers you invite can read your records. Data is encrypted in transit.

## Sharing with caregivers

If you invite a partner or caregiver to a baby's profile, they can see and add records for that baby. You can revoke access at any time from the app.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can permanently delete your account and **all** associated data at any time from **Profile → Delete Account** in the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

## Children's privacy

TinyTrack is intended for use by parents and caregivers (adults). Baby records are entered by and belong to the account holder.
